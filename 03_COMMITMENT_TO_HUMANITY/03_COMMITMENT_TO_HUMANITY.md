## Human-Centered Design

OLCA is built with human welfare as a primary design constraint. In the carbon capture, utilization, and storage domain, this means:

- **Accessibility:** Designed for diverse user populations
- **Privacy:** Data minimization and purpose limitation
- **Fairness:** Bias detection and mitigation in AI systems
- **Accountability:** Clear chains of responsibility for AI decisions

## Ethical Framework

The project adheres to the Anticloud ethical framework:
1. Do no harm
2. Respect human autonomy
3. Ensure transparency
4. Maintain accountability
